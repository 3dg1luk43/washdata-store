Automation templates

Ready-made automations

Home Assistant blueprints that act when an appliance starts, finishes or needs unloading. Each one becomes a normal automation you can edit.

In WashData 0.5.9 or newer, the panel's Automations tab adds these to an appliance in one click. It needs WashData's online features turned on. Without them, use Import with My Home Assistant.
